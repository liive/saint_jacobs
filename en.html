Saint Jacob’s brings a fresh take on Basque cooking to central Vilnius. Seafood, dishes from the grill and food made for sharing come together in a kitchen that approaches tradition with curiosity.

Join us for dinner for two or settle in for an evening with friends. Order a little of this and that, share what arrives and discover your own taste of the Basque Country.
